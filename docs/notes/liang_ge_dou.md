
# Both with 两个都

In Mandarin, “both” often means “the two known people or things.” A common simple pattern is **两个都**.

| Chinese | Pinyin | Meaning |
| ------- | ------ | ------- |
| 两个     | liǎng gè | two; two of them |
| 都       | dōu      | both; all         |
| 两个都   | liǎng gè dōu | both of the two |

!!! tip
    **两个** means “two.”  
    **两个都** means “both.”

## Basic pattern

Use **两个都** when there are already two known people or things.

```text
两个都 + verb / adjective
````

!!! example "Both are good"
    {{zh:两个都好。}}  
    Liǎng gè dōu hǎo.  
    Both are good.  


    The speaker already knows which two things are being discussed.


!!! example "Both are here"
    {{zh:两个都在。}}  
    Liǎng gè dōu zài.  
    Both are here.  


    **都** comes before the verb or adjective.


!!! example "Both came"
    {{zh:两个都来了。}}  
    Liǎng gè dōu lái le.  
    Both came.  


    **两个都** refers to the two known people.


## With pronouns

You can put a pronoun before **两个都**.

 * 他们两个都 + verb / adjective
 * 她们两个都 + verb / adjective
 * 我们两个都 + verb / adjective

!!! example "Both of them came"
    {{zh:他们两个都来了。}}  
    Tāmen liǎng gè dōu lái le.  
    Both of them came.  


    **他们两个** means “the two of them.”


!!! example "Both of them are students"
    {{zh:她们两个都是学生。}}  
    Tāmen liǎng gè dōu shì xuésheng.  
    Both of them are students.  


    **都** comes before **是**.


!!! example "Both of us want tea"
    {{zh:我们两个都要茶。}}  
    Wǒmen liǎng gè dōu yào chá.  
    Both of us want tea.  


    **我们两个** means “the two of us.”


## With 这 and 那

 * Use **这两个** for “these two.”
 * Use **那两个** for “those two.”

!!! example "These two are good"
    {{zh:这两个都好。}}  
    Zhè liǎng gè dōu hǎo.  
    These two are both good.


    **这两个** means “these two.”


!!! example "Those two are expensive"
    {{zh:那两个都贵。}}  
    Nà liǎng gè dōu guì.  
    Those two are both expensive.


    **都** comes before the adjective **贵**.


!!! example "These two are students"
    {{zh:这两个都是学生。}}  
    Zhè liǎng gè dōu shì xuésheng.  
    These two are both students.


    With **是**, put **都** before **是**.


## Saying “I want both”

In English, “both” can be the object: “I want both.”

In Mandarin, **都** usually comes before the verb. So the known two things often come before **我都要**.

!!! example "I want both of these two"
    {{zh:这两个我都要。}}  
    Zhè liǎng gè wǒ dōu yào.  
    I want both of these two.


    Literally: “These two, I both want.”


!!! example "I want both of those two"
    {{zh:那两个我都要。}}  
    Nà liǎng gè wǒ dōu yào.  
    I want both of those two.


    **那两个** is placed first because it is already known.


!!! example "I want both"
    {{zh:两个我都要。}}  
    Liǎng gè wǒ dōu yào.  
    I want both.


    This is used when the two things are already clear.


!!! example "I want both"
    {{zh:我两个都要。}}  
    Wǒ liǎng gè dōu yào.  
    I want both.


    This is a compact spoken form. The meaning is still “I want both of the two known things.”


## Two vs both

 * **两个** can just mean “two.”
 * **两个都** means “both.”

!!! example "I want two"
    {{zh:我要两个。}}  
    Wǒ yào liǎng gè.  
    I want two.


    This only says the number is two.


!!! example "I want both"
    {{zh:这两个我都要。}}  
    Zhè liǎng gè wǒ dōu yào.  
    I want both of these two.


    This says there are two known things, and the speaker wants both.


## Negative form

To say “neither,” use **两个都不** before the verb or adjective.

!!! example "Neither is good"
    {{zh:两个都不好。}}  
    Liǎng gè dōu bù hǎo.  
    Neither is good.


    Literally: “Both are not good.”


!!! example "I do not want either"
    {{zh:这两个我都不要。}}  
    Zhè liǎng gè wǒ dōu bù yào.  
    I do not want either of these two.


    Literally: “These two, I both do not want.”


## Main patterns

| Pattern                  | Example        | Meaning                       |
| ------------------------ | -------------- | ----------------------------- |
| 两个都 + adjective          | {{zh:两个都好。}}   | Both are good.                |
| 两个都 + verb               | {{zh:两个都来了。}}  | Both came.                    |
| Pronoun + 两个都            | {{zh:他们两个都来。}} | Both of them are coming.      |
| 这两个都                     | {{zh:这两个都好。}}  | These two are both good.      |
| 那两个都                     | {{zh:那两个都贵。}}  | Those two are both expensive. |
| 这两个 + subject + 都 + verb | {{zh:这两个我都要。}} | I want both of these two.     |
| 两个都不                     | {{zh:两个都不好。}}  | Neither is good.              |

!!! tip
**都** normally comes before the verb or adjective.
