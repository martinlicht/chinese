**早上好** means “good morning,” but in daily speech many people simply say **早**.

**我从……来** literally means “I came from ...” and often refers to the place you came from physically, not necessarily your hometown or nationality.

!!! example
你是德国人吗？
Nǐ shì Déguó rén ma?
Are you German?

!!! example
你是美国人吗？
Nǐ shì Měiguó rén ma?
Are you American?

!!! example
你是老师吗？
Nǐ shì lǎoshī ma?
Are you a teacher?
