

Chinese movement verbs are often combined with **direction complements** like 来, 去, 上, 下, 进, 出, 回, 过, 起.

## Basic movement verbs

| Chinese |  Pinyin | Meaning             | German                    |
| ------- | ------: | ------------------- | ------------------------- |
| 去       |      qù | to go               | gehen, hingehen           |
| 来       |     lái | to come             | kommen                    |
| 走       |     zǒu | to walk; to leave   | gehen, weggehen           |
| 跑       |     pǎo | to run              | laufen, rennen            |
| 进       |     jìn | to enter            | hineingehen               |
| 出       |     chū | to exit             | hinausgehen               |
| 回       |     huí | to return           | zurückgehen               |
| 上       |   shàng | to go up; get on    | hinaufgehen, einsteigen   |
| 下       |     xià | to go down; get off | hinuntergehen, aussteigen |
| 过       |     guò | to cross; pass      | überqueren, vorbeigehen   |
| 到       |     dào | to arrive at        | ankommen in/bei           |
| 离开      |   líkāi | to leave            | verlassen                 |
| 经过      | jīngguò | to pass through/by  | vorbeikommen, durchqueren |
| 参观      | cānguān | to visit/tour       | besichtigen               |

## Core patterns

### 去 / 来 + place

我去学校。
**Wǒ qù xuéxiào.**
I go to school. / Ich gehe zur Schule.

他来我家。
**Tā lái wǒ jiā.**
He comes to my home. / Er kommt zu mir nach Hause.

### 到 + place

我到北京了。
**Wǒ dào Běijīng le.**
I arrived in Beijing. / Ich bin in Peking angekommen.

我们几点到？
**Wǒmen jǐ diǎn dào?**
When do we arrive? / Wann kommen wir an?

## Useful “prepositions” / coverbs

Chinese often uses **coverbs**, which behave partly like prepositions and partly like verbs.

| Chinese |   Pinyin | Meaning                  | German             |
| ------- | -------: | ------------------------ | ------------------ |
| 从       |     cóng | from                     | von, aus           |
| 到       |      dào | to, until                | bis, nach          |
| 往       |     wǎng | toward                   | in Richtung        |
| 向       |    xiàng | toward                   | zu, in Richtung    |
| 朝       |     cháo | facing/toward            | in Richtung        |
| 在       |      zài | at, in, on               | in, an, auf, bei   |
| 离       |       lí | away from; distance from | von … entfernt     |
| 经过      |  jīngguò | through/by               | durch, an … vorbei |
| 通过      |  tōngguò | through, via             | durch, mittels     |

| 顺着      |  shùnzhe | following/along          | entlang            |
| 穿过      | chuānguò | through/across           | durchqueren        |
| 过       |      guò | across, past             | über, vorbei       |

## Important structures

### 从 A 到 B

从北京到上海
**cóng Běijīng dào Shànghǎi**
from Beijing to Shanghai

我从家到学校走路二十分钟。
**Wǒ cóng jiā dào xuéxiào zǒulù èrshí fēnzhōng.**
I walk twenty minutes from home to school.

### 往 / 向 / 朝 + direction

往左走。
**Wǎng zuǒ zǒu.**
Go left.

向前走。
**Xiàng qián zǒu.**
Go forward.

朝我这边走。
**Cháo wǒ zhè biān zǒu.**
Walk toward me.

### 在 + place + verb

我在公园散步。
**Wǒ zài gōngyuán sànbù.**
I am walking in the park.

他在路上跑步。
**Tā zài lù shang pǎobù.**
He is running on the road.

### 离 + place + distance

我家离学校很近。
**Wǒ jiā lí xuéxiào hěn jìn.**
My home is close to the school.

火车站离这里远吗？
**Huǒchēzhàn lí zhèlǐ yuǎn ma?**
Is the train station far from here?

## Useful conversation questions

你怎么来这里的？
**Nǐ zěnme lái zhèlǐ de?**
How did you get here?

你平时怎么去上班/上课？
**Nǐ píngshí zěnme qù shàngbān/shàngkè?**
How do you usually go to work/class?

从你家到市中心要多久？
**Cóng nǐ jiā dào shìzhōngxīn yào duōjiǔ?**
How long does it take from your home to the city center?

你喜欢走路还是骑自行车？
**Nǐ xǐhuān zǒulù háishi qí zìxíngchē?**
Do you prefer walking or cycling?

你最近去了哪里？
**Nǐ zuìjìn qù le nǎlǐ?**
Where have you gone recently?

你什么时候回德国/中国？
**Nǐ shénme shíhou huí Déguó/Zhōngguó?**
When are you going back to Germany/China?

## Subtle distinctions worth discussing

**来** means movement toward the speaker or reference point.
**去** means movement away from the speaker or reference point.

他进来了。
He came in. The speaker is inside.

他进去了。
He went in. The speaker is outside.

**走** can mean “walk,” but often means “leave.”

我走了。
I’m leaving.

**到** emphasizes arrival.

我去了北京。
I went to Beijing.

我到了北京。
I arrived in Beijing.

**回** means return to a previous or expected place.

我回家。
I go home.

我回德国。
I return to Germany.
