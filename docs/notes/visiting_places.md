
# Visiting places

Let's talk about four big cities in China


 City | Pinyin   |
------------------
 北京 | Běijīng   |
 上海 | Shànghǎi  |
 深圳 | Shēnzhèn  |
 重庆 | Chóngqìng |


We want to visit the cities and talk to the people there. The useful patterns are the following.

**我去 + city。**
I go to city.

**我要去 + city。**
I want to go to city. / I am going to city.

**我在 + city。**
I am in city.

**我住在 + city。**
I live in city.

**我在 + city + 工作。**
I work in city.

**我在 + city + 学习。**
I study in city.

**我去过 + city。**
I have been to city.

**我没去过 + city。**
I have not been to city.

**我了解 + city。**
I know / understand city.

**我熟悉 + city。**
I am familiar with city.

**我对 + city + 很熟悉。**
I am very familiar with city.

**我不太了解 + city。**
I do not know city very well.






##  I am in Beijing







**我在北京。**
“I am in Beijing.”

Structure: **我** = I, **在** = be located at/in, **北京** = Beijing.
Here **在** marks location.

---

**我走过北京。**
“I have walked through Beijing.”
Or: “I have passed through Beijing on foot.”

Structure: **走过** = have walked through / have passed through.
**过** after a verb often means an experience has happened before.

This sentence emphasizes that Beijing was crossed or passed through.

---

**我要走北京。**
This is unnatural Chinese in most contexts.

Literally: “I want to walk Beijing.”
But **走北京** is not the normal way to say “walk around Beijing” or “go to Beijing.”

Natural alternatives:

**我要去北京。**
“I want to go to Beijing.”

**我要离开北京。**
“I am going to leave Beijing.”

**我要在北京走走。**
“I want to walk around in Beijing.”

**我要逛北京。**
“I want to tour/explore Beijing.”

---

**你在北京做什么？**
“What are you doing in Beijing?”
Or: “What do you do in Beijing?”

Structure: **你** = you, **在北京** = in Beijing, **做什么** = do what.

Depending on context, it can mean either:

1. “What are you doing in Beijing right now?”
2. “What is your work/activity in Beijing?”



Here are learner-friendly examples around **visiting / living in / knowing Beijing**.

## Visiting Beijing

**我去北京。**
*wǒ qù Běijīng*
I go to Beijing.

**我要去北京。**
*wǒ yào qù Běijīng*
I want to go to Beijing. / I am going to Beijing.

**我想去北京旅游。**
*wǒ xiǎng qù Běijīng lǚyóu*
I want to travel to Beijing.

**我来北京旅游。**
*wǒ lái Běijīng lǚyóu*
I came to Beijing to travel.

**我到了北京。**
*wǒ dào le Běijīng*
I arrived in Beijing.

**我在北京玩了三天。**
*wǒ zài Běijīng wán le sān tiān*
I spent three days having fun in Beijing.

**我参观了故宫。**
*wǒ cānguān le Gùgōng*
I visited the Forbidden City.

**我爬了长城。**
*wǒ pá le Chángchéng*
I climbed the Great Wall.

**北京很好玩。**
*Běijīng hěn hǎowán*
Beijing is very fun / interesting.






















## Living in Beijing

**我在北京。**
*wǒ zài Běijīng*
I am in Beijing.

**我住在北京。**
*wǒ zhù zài Běijīng*
I live in Beijing.

**我住在北京的海淀区。**
*wǒ zhù zài Běijīng de Hǎidiàn Qū*
I live in Beijing’s Haidian District.

**我在北京工作。**
*wǒ zài Běijīng gōngzuò*
I work in Beijing.

**我在北京学习中文。**
*wǒ zài Běijīng xuéxí Zhōngwén*
I study Chinese in Beijing.

**我已经在北京住了两年。**
*wǒ yǐjīng zài Běijīng zhù le liǎng nián*
I have already lived in Beijing for two years.

**我以前住在北京。**
*wǒ yǐqián zhù zài Běijīng*
I used to live in Beijing.

**我不住在北京，我住在上海。**
*wǒ bú zhù zài Běijīng, wǒ zhù zài Shànghǎi*
I do not live in Beijing. I live in Shanghai.

## Knowing Beijing

**我认识北京。**
*wǒ rènshi Běijīng*
This is understandable, but not very natural for a city. It can mean “I know Beijing,” but sounds less precise.

Better:

**我了解北京。**
*wǒ liǎojiě Běijīng*
I know Beijing / I understand Beijing.

**我很了解北京。**
*wǒ hěn liǎojiě Běijīng*
I know Beijing well.

**我不太了解北京。**
*wǒ bú tài liǎojiě Běijīng*
I do not know Beijing very well.

**我熟悉北京。**
*wǒ shúxī Běijīng*
I am familiar with Beijing.

**我对北京很熟悉。**
*wǒ duì Běijīng hěn shúxī*
I am very familiar with Beijing.

**我知道北京是中国的首都。**
*wǒ zhīdào Běijīng shì Zhōngguó de shǒudū*
I know that Beijing is the capital of China.

**我听说北京很大。**
*wǒ tīngshuō Běijīng hěn dà*
I heard that Beijing is very big.

## Useful questions

**你去过北京吗？**
*nǐ qù guo Běijīng ma*
Have you been to Beijing?

**你在北京住过吗？**
*nǐ zài Běijīng zhù guo ma*
Have you ever lived in Beijing?

**你住在北京吗？**
*nǐ zhù zài Běijīng ma*
Do you live in Beijing?

**你什么时候去北京？**
*nǐ shénme shíhou qù Běijīng*
When are you going to Beijing?

**你为什么来北京？**
*nǐ wèishénme lái Běijīng*
Why did you come to Beijing?

**你对北京熟悉吗？**
*nǐ duì Běijīng shúxī ma*
Are you familiar with Beijing?

**在北京** = in Beijing

I came to Beijing to study.

**住在北京** = live in Beijing

I live in Beijing.

**去过北京** = have been to Beijing
